🧪 HOW TO TEST VENDOR ONBOARDING
===================================

QUICK START (30 seconds):
-------------------------
1. Look at TOP-RIGHT corner of screen
2. Click the dropdown button (shows current mode)
3. Select "🚀 Vendor Onboarding"
4. You're in! Start testing.


COMPLETE TESTING FLOW (5 minutes):
----------------------------------

Step 1: REGISTER
  - Fill in business name, country, email, password
  - Click "Create account"
  - ✅ Should see dashboard with 0% progress

Step 2: COMPLETE SETUP (any order)
  - Click "Restaurant Profile" → Fill form → Save
  - Click "Menu Setup" → Add category → Add items → Save  
  - Click "Tables & QR" → Add tables → Notice QR preview watermark
  - ✅ Progress bar should show 100%

Step 3: TRY TO ACTIVATE (PAYWALL TEST)
  - Click big green "Activate restaurant" button
  - ✅ Should see subscription gate (HARD STOP)
  - ❌ Cannot bypass this screen

Step 4: SUBSCRIBE (STRIPE CHECKOUT FLOW)
  - Click "Subscribe & go live" on Monthly or Yearly
  - ✅ Backend creates checkout session
  - ✅ Shows "Redirecting to checkout..." button
  - ✅ URL changes with session_id parameter
  - ✅ Shows "Processing payment..." spinner
  - ✅ Auto-verifies payment (2 seconds in demo mode)

Step 5: VERIFY ACTIVATION
  - ✅ Should see "You're Live! 🎉" screen
  - Notice "Active" status badge
  - Try downloading QR codes (should work now)
  - ✅ Vendor is fully activated


ADMIN MONITORING:
-----------------
1. Click mode switcher dropdown
2. Select "🛠️ Vendor Management"
3. See all vendors in table
4. Try filtering by status
5. Click "View" or "Suspend" buttons
