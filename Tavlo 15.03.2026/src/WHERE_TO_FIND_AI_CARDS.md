## 🔍 How to Spot the Purple Card

### Visual Markers (Same Everywhere):
1. **Purple gradient** background (light to lighter purple)
2. **✨ Sparkle icon** (purple) on left side
3. **"AI Review Summary"** heading
4. **Sentiment badge** (POSITIVE/MIXED/NEGATIVE)
5. **Checkmarks ✓** with percentages
6. **Rounded corners** and subtle border

### Position:
- Customer dish reviews: **FIRST card** before individual reviews
- Admin vendor modal: **After rating bars**, before recent reviews
- Restaurant reviews tab: **TOP of right column**, before helpful reviews
- Restaurant modal: **At top**, before review list
- Vendor dashboard: **Always at top**, before filter tabs

Each purple card shows:
- **Sentiment** - Overall mood (POSITIVE/MIXED/NEGATIVE)
